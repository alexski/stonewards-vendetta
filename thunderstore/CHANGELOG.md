# Changelog

## 0.2.1

## 0.2.0
- Vendetta director: one monster per wave hunts a dwarf, with stacking damage, slower heavier hits, healing when ignored
- Leaps onto the wall with a telegraph and follows its target along it and back down; the courtyard is safe for a short while, less every wave; goes for the King while its target is dead; waits at the mine entrance and grows while its target hides underground
- Greed waves target the top miner
- Revive-or-lose (or instant loss), rewards for avenging and early kills
- Nemesis system: when a vendetta monster downs its target, one of its wave returns in a later run with a perk and carried stacks
- 25 s revive window that stops once the body is on a revive spot; rewards are paid in iron
- On-screen marker, announcements, countdowns, debug overlay
- Other players with the mod see the host's marker, announcements and countdowns
- Works with BepInEx.ConfigurationManager and Stonewards ModSettings
